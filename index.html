About Me

Hi, I'm Madhan Aadithya — a singer, songwriter, and storyteller from Tambaram.

I write songs about love, heartbreak, late-night drives, small-town dreams, and everything that makes us feel alive.

My music is a mix of acoustic warmth, pop melodies, and honest lyrics — the kind of songs you play when the world feels too loud or too quiet.
