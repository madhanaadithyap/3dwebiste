About

Madhan Aadithya is a composer and musician from Tambaram, weaving emotions through melody, rhythm, and silence.

Trained in Carnatic and contemporary styles, his work spans film scores, independent albums, live performances, and cross-cultural collaborations.

Music, for him, is meditation — a bridge between tradition and tomorrow.
